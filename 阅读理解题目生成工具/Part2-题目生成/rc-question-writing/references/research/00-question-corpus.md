### 写作手法 · 开篇引入(3 题,固定 Q1)

**1-写作手法/风暴追逐(How does the writer lead in the topic?)**
a. By asking questions.  b. By telling a story.  c. By listing numbers.  d. By giving an example.
→ 答:a。首段两个问句引题(Are you afraid of big storms?...But did you know...?)

**1-写作手法/气味手机(How does the writer begin the article to attract the readers?)**
a. By explaining the scientific background.  b. By describing a possible future situation.  c. By listing challenges in creating smell phones.  d. By introducing a real-life app of smell phones.
→ 答:b。首段 "IMAGINE THIS:...you can also smell the cookies..." 描述未来场景。

**1-写作手法/自动驾驶(How does the writer begin the passage?)**
a. By telling a moving story.  b. By describing a clear picture.  c. By giving a surprising number.  d. By comparing two different ideas.
→ 答:b。首段 "Imagine this: a car driving through busy city traffic, but with no one in the driver's seat."
